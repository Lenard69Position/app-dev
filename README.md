## 💻 Tech & Production Trivia
The filmmakers had to employ specialized driving techniques and production tricks to pull off the visuals:
```text
- Over 100 cars were destroyed or heavily damaged during the high-speed stunt filming.
- Real drift legends like Keiichi Tsuchiya (the actual real-life "Drift King") served as stunt coordinators and made a cameo appearance.
- Thousands of tires were burned through to capture the heavy smoke aesthetic during the drift sequences.
```
